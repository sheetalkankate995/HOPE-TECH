Together Towards Awareness & Innovation. An educational machine learning project designed to explore suicidal ideation signals in social media text using Machine Learning and Genetic Algorithms.

About HOPE TECH

HOPE TECH is our final-year academic project. It explores how machine learning techniques can support research into identifying concerning language patterns in social media text.

Our Mission

To explore machine learning approaches for understanding potentially concerning language patterns in online text.

Our Vision

To encourage awareness, responsible technology development and research using artificial intelligence.

What We Do

We study social media text using machine learning models and compare their experimental results.

Our Technology

Our project uses Python, Flask, Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing and Genetic Algorithms.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to explore how technology can contribute to awareness and academic research. We believe responsible innovation and human understanding should go together.

Educational Disclaimer: HOPE TECH is an academic research prototype. Its predictions are experimental and are not a medical diagnosis, clinical assessment or substitute for professional mental health support.
